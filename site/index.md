Things I publish: demonstrations, tutorials, summaries and lists, most of them written with LLMs (Claude and others) and reviewed by me.

- [Library](library/index.html): longer documents — study guides, digests and preparation notes, readable here and downloadable as EPUB, PDF or Markdown.
- [Explainers](explainers/index.html): explanations of books, concepts and facts, worked through with examples and the parts the source leaves out.
- [AI tells](ai-tells/): a dated catalogue of the signs that give AI-generated text, images, video, audio and design away — which ones still work and which newer models have outgrown — and a quiz: can you tell human-made from AI-made?
