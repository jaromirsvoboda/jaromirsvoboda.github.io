Things I publish: demonstrations, tutorials, summaries and lists, most of them written with LLMs (Claude and others) and reviewed by me.

- [Library](library/index.html): study guides, digests and preparation notes, readable here and downloadable as EPUB, PDF or Markdown.
- [Explainers](explainers/index.html): explanations of books, concepts and facts, worked through with examples and the parts the source leaves out.
- [AI tells](ai-tells/): the signs that give away AI-made text, pictures, video, sound and design, dated, with the ones newer models no longer show. There is also a quiz: can you tell what a person made from what a model made?
- [Poster review](poster-review/): flyer drafts for the PauseAI project, in a public gallery, organized by review round.
