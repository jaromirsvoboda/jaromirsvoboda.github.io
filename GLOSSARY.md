# jaromirsvoboda.github.io

A personal site of things published from the vault: longer documents and explanations, mostly written with LLMs and reviewed by the owner.

## Language

### Content

**Section**:
A top-level part of the site with its own generated index, such as Library or Explainers.
_Avoid_: category, collection

**Library document**:
A standalone longer piece (a study guide, digest or preparation notes) that is read on its own.
_Avoid_: article, post

**Explainer**:
A page that teaches concepts from a **Source**, organised by topic, with worked examples and the gaps the source leaves.
_Avoid_: summary, notes, tutorial

**Source**:
The book, paper or other work an **Explainer** explains, cited by section number and linked (and rehosted when its licence allows).
_Avoid_: reference, original

**Downloads**:
The EPUB, A4 PDF and Markdown files offered beside a page, plus a rehosted **Source** where there is one.

### Publishing

**Publish**:
Moving content from the vault into the site repository so that it becomes public history.
_Avoid_: deploy (that is the later build and upload by GitHub Actions)

**Leak gate**:
A mandatory check that runs before anything is published and refuses personal, confidential or secret content.
_Avoid_: leak scan (that is one tool inside the gate)
